Getting started

No. The planner on this site runs entirely in your browser and stores your trip in local storage on your own device. No account, no email, no card. An account only becomes useful when you want the trip on more than one device, want to share it with somebody, or want more than one trip saved at a time.

No. It is permanent. Two active trips, up to fourteen days each, full day-plan cards with travel times and costs, three packing checklist templates, and browser printing. It does not expire, it does not require a card, and we do not email you asking you to upgrade.

About forty-five minutes for a first-time user, and closer to twenty once you know the tool. The slow part is deciding what you want to do, not entering it. Copying one of our published itineraries and adapting it takes about ten minutes.

Yes. The planner works in any current mobile browser and the layout is built for a 360 pixel wide screen upward. Most people build the plan on a laptop and use it on a phone, which is what the printed and PDF versions are for.

On the free browser planner, the trip goes with it, because nothing is stored on our servers. That is the trade for requiring no account. If losing a plan would be a problem, either export it to PDF or use a Hardpack account where trips are stored server-side and backed up.

Plans and billing

$9 per month or $79 per year. It adds unlimited trips and days, unlimited offline PDF export, six collaborators per trip, all eleven checklist templates, flight and hotel price alerts, map view, and a one business day support target. The full comparison is on the pricing page.

They stay. Every trip becomes read-only and remains printable and exportable for as long as your account exists. Nothing is deleted. If you resubscribe later, everything becomes editable again exactly as you left it.

Yes, within fourteen days of the first charge on a new Pro subscription, in full, no questions. Annual plans cancelled after that window are refunded on a prorated basis for complete unused months. Custom itinerary orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before we start work. Full terms are on the refund and cancellation page.

Where a US state requires sales tax on digital subscriptions, it is calculated at checkout from your billing address and shown before you confirm. The prices we advertise are pre-tax.

Yes. $79 a year against $108 if you pay monthly, so annual saves $29. We also give free Pro accounts to accredited outdoor education programs and to volunteer search and rescue teams. Email us from your organization address.

The custom itinerary service

A day-by-day plan built by a planner on our team and reviewed by a second planner, covering up to fourteen days in one country or one contiguous region. It includes time-stamped stops, travel times, daily costs in USD, lodging options at three price points, a packing list, and a printable PDF. It arrives in your Hardpack account as an editable trip.

No. We are not a travel agency, a tour operator or a seller of travel. We do not hold your money, we do not make reservations on your behalf, and we do not take responsibility for supplier bookings. You book everything yourself, which also means you keep any loyalty benefits and elite status.

Five business days for the first draft and two more for one round of revisions. If you have a departure inside two weeks, tell us in the request and we will say honestly whether we can meet it before you pay.

One round of revisions is included. If the revised plan still does not work for you, tell us within seven days of delivery and we refund the fee in full. We have done this and we would rather refund than argue.

Trust, ads and affiliates

Five ways, in order of size: Pro subscriptions, affiliate commissions from hotel, flight, car rental and tour partners, the custom itinerary service, agency and white-label plans, and clearly labeled advertising. There is a plain-English breakdown on the home page, the pricing page and the about page.

No. Where we list lodging or tours, the sort order is distance, price or rating and the sort you chose is always visible. A higher commission does not move anything up a list, and it does not put a hotel into an itinerary. Planners are not compensated on bookings. The full policy is in our affiliate disclosure.

Yes. Every quote on the reviews page comes from a named customer who gave written permission to publish it. We do not write them, we do not pay for them, we do not edit them for tone, and we do not remove the critical ones. Our methodology is described at the bottom of that page.

Not at the moment. There are no display ad units anywhere on this site. If we introduce them, they will be clearly labeled, kept off legal pages and the 404 page, kept out of the planner workspace, and never placed above the main content of a page. Advertising cookies are not set unless you accept them in the cookie banner, whether or not ads are running.

Privacy, data and accessibility

No, and that includes the California definition of sharing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The Do Not Sell or Share link in the footer opens your cookie choices and lets you opt out of advertising cookies at any time.

For the browser planner, nothing leaves your device. For an account, we hold your email, your trips, and billing records handled by our payment processor. Analytics and advertising cookies only run if you accept them. Details, retention periods and how to make a request are in the privacy policy.

We target WCAG 2.1 Level AA. That means semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus states, keyboard operation for every control, alt text on every image, a 4.5:1 minimum text contrast ratio, and full support for prefers-reduced-motion which disables the crosshair, the marquee and snap scrolling. If something does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it. See the accessibility statement.

Yes. The Grid button in the sidebar switches the baseline grid overlay off and remembers your choice in this browser. Reduced-motion settings are respected automatically and do not need a toggle.
