How we collect and publish reviews

Anyone who finishes a trip built in Hardpack gets one email asking two questions: what worked, and what did not. That is the entire collection method. There is no incentive, no discount, no prize draw, and no follow-up if you ignore it.

What we publish: the full response, unedited except for trimming to length, with the customer's name, city and trip. We ask for written permission before anything appears here and we accept a first name only if that is what somebody prefers.

What we do not do: we do not write reviews, buy reviews, pay for reviews, offer anything in exchange for a review, or remove a review because it is critical. Two of the twelve on this page are below 4.5 and they are staying.

Corrections we made because of these: the Norway budget table on our destinations page was revised upward in July 2025 after Nadia sent us her actual receipts. Offline maps, which Yolanda asked for, are not on our roadmap, and we would rather say that than imply otherwise.
