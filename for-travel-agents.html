N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 01 · THE CASE

What this replaces

Most independent agents and small operators build client itineraries in a word processor, price them in a spreadsheet, and send them as a PDF attachment that goes out of date the moment a flight changes.

Hardpack replaces that with a single document your client can open on a phone at the gate. You edit it, they see the change. It carries your logo, your colors and, on the Outfitter plan, your own domain.

The economics we hear back: agents tell us a custom multi-stop itinerary takes four to six hours to produce in a word processor and about ninety minutes here, mostly because the day totals, drive times and cost lines maintain themselves. At $89 a month for five seats, the plan pays for itself on the first itinerary of the month.

What we do not do: we are software, not a host agency. We do not take a cut of your commissions, we do not sell travel, we do not contact your clients, and we do not put our own affiliate links inside your client-facing itineraries. Your client sees your brand and your links.

Who uses it
Independent agents, small host-agency teams, adventure and hiking operators, corporate retreat planners, destination wedding planners

N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 03 · ONBOARDING

What the first two weeks look like

01

Setup call, 45 minutes

We look at two itineraries you have already sent to clients, and rebuild them live in the tool so you can see the difference in output before you commit anything.

02

Brand load

You send a logo file and two hex colors. We configure the itinerary template, the PDF header and, on Outfitter, the domain and SSL. Takes us two business days.

03

Template build

We turn your two most-sold trips into reusable templates so your team can duplicate and adjust rather than starting from an empty day.

04

Team training

One 30 minute session for the whole team, recorded so new staff can watch it later. After that it is email or phone support at the tier you bought.
