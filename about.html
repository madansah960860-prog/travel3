Why this exists

In September 2019 the two of us drove Iceland's Ring Road in five days because a spreadsheet said it was possible. It was possible. It was also 250 miles a day, three arguments, and one waterfall we saw entirely from a car window.

The spreadsheet was not wrong about distance. It was wrong about everything else: parking, the walk from the lot, the twenty minutes lost finding the trailhead, the fact that a gas station sandwich in Iceland costs $14 and there are only two of them in 80 miles.

We started Hardpack in April 2021 to build the tool we wanted on that drive. The core idea has not changed since the first version: a plan is only useful if it carries the boring numbers. What time does it open, how long does it take to get there, what does it cost per person, and what happens if it rains.

We are four people. We are not venture funded, we do not have a growth team, and there is nobody here whose job is to make you click something. Pro subscriptions are the majority of our revenue, which means the incentive is to make the tool good enough that people keep paying $9 a month, rather than to sell your attention to somebody else.

We are based in Portland, Oregon, which is a good place to test a product for people who want to be outside and keep getting rained on.

N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 03 · PRINCIPLES

Five things we will not do

01

Sell your personal information

Not to data brokers, not to advertisers, not to anyone. This is written into the privacy policy and it includes the California definition of "share" for cross-context behavioral advertising.

02

Rank by commission

Where we show lodging or tour options, the order is distance, price or rating, and the sort you chose is always visible on screen. A higher commission never moves anything up the list.

03

Use fake urgency

No countdown timers, no "4 people are viewing this", no invented scarcity. If a permit lottery genuinely closes on a date, we print the date and link to the government page that says so.

04

Publish a review we cannot verify

Every quote on the reviews page comes from a named customer who gave written permission. We do not edit them for tone and we do not remove the critical ones.

05

Hold your trips hostage

Cancel Pro and your trips stay. They become read-only and printable and they remain that way for as long as your account exists. You can export everything you have ever built to CSV and PDF at any time, including after cancelling. Software that deletes your work when you stop paying is not software, it is a hostage situation.

The business model, plainly

Freemium software plus four smaller lines. We publish this on the home page, the pricing page and here, because it is the question people are really asking when they ask whether a free tool is trustworthy.

  1. Pro subscriptions, $9 a month or $79 a year. The majority of revenue.
  2. Affiliate commissions from hotel, flight, car rental and tour partners. You pay the same price as booking direct. Disclosed at the point of use and in full on our affiliate disclosure.
  3. Custom itinerary service, $149 or $249 one time.
  4. Agency and white-label plans, $89 to $249 a month.
  5. Advertising. Not currently running on this site. If it ever does, it will be labeled and confined to editorial pages.
