Affiliate Disclosure

We earn commissions from some links on this site. Here is exactly how that works, what it changes, and what it does not.

Effective date: 06/01/2026 Last updated: 06/01/2026 Hardpack Trip Co. Portland, Oregon, USA

Please note: this is a starting template written for our business. Have a licensed attorney review and adapt it before relying on it for yours. Questions about this page go to hello@hardpack.travel.

N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 01 · AFFILIATE

Full text

The short version

Some links on hardpack.travel are affiliate links. If you click one and make a booking or purchase, the company may pay us a commission. You pay the same price you would pay going direct. Commission never changes what we recommend, and we label affiliate links wherever they appear.

1. Why this page exists

The US Federal Trade Commission requires clear and conspicuous disclosure of material connections between a publisher and the products it writes about. A commission is a material connection. This page is our full disclosure, and we also flag affiliate links at the point of use so you do not have to come here to find out.

2. What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link is a normal link with a tracking identifier attached. When you click it, the partner's network sets a cookie in your browser on their own domain. If you book within their attribution window, typically 24 hours to 30 days, the partner credits the booking to us and pays a commission out of their own margin.

The commission comes from the partner, not from you. Affiliate pricing is the same as the public price. We have never had access to, and would not use, a mechanism that raises a price because you arrived from here.

4. What commission does not buy

  • It does not change ranking. Where we list options, the sort order is distance, price or rating, and the sort you selected is always visible on screen.
  • It does not put a hotel or tour into an itinerary. Every recommendation is chosen by a planner on editorial grounds first, and only then checked for whether an affiliate program happens to exist.
  • It does not affect the custom itinerary service. Planners are salaried and are not compensated on bookings.
  • It does not appear in agency client itineraries. Travel agents on Studio and Outfitter plans send itineraries with their own links. We never insert ours.
  • It does not stop us publishing something negative. If a place is not worth the money we say so, including for partners we earn from.

5. How we label affiliate links

Wherever an affiliate link appears in a guide or itinerary, a short labeled note appears in the same section explaining that the link may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Sponsored content, if we ever publish any, would be labeled "Sponsored" in the headline area, and we have not published any to date.

We do not use affiliate links inside the trip planner workspace or on any legal page.

6. Advertising is separate

Advertising units on this site are labeled "Advertisement" and are served by an advertising network. They are not editorial recommendations and we do not choose which specific ads appear. Advertising cookies are only set if you accept them in the cookie settings.

7. Your choices

You are never required to use our links. If you prefer, search for the supplier directly and book there. It costs you the same and it costs us the commission, which is your call to make.

If you want to avoid affiliate tracking entirely, clear cookies before booking, or use a private browsing window.
