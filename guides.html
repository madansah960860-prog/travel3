How we research and update these

Every guide on this site is written by a named person on our team who has planned or taken the trip. We are a four person company, so there is no content farm behind this and no articles bought by the word.

What we verify before publishing: prices against the operator's own site, opening hours against the official source, and permit or reservation rules against the government agency that runs them. Where a number is our own estimate rather than a published figure, we say so in the text.

How often we update: destination pages and permit guides get a full review every January and a spot check when a park service or tourism board announces a change. The review date is printed at the top of each guide.

Corrections. If something here is wrong, tell us and we will fix it and note the change. Send a correction.

Affiliate links. Some links in these guides earn us a commission. They never change what we recommend, and every guide that contains one says so where it appears. Read the full disclosure.

Get the new ones by email

Roughly one email a month when a guide publishes or gets a significant update. No daily deals, no partner blasts, unsubscribe in one click.
