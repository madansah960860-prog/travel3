N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 03 · DONE FOR YOU

Custom itinerary service

A flat one-time fee. A planner on our team builds the trip, a second planner reviews it, and you own the file in your account like any other trip.

This is not a subscription and it does not require one. It exists because a meaningful number of people would rather pay once than learn a tool.

Single country or region
$149 one time. Up to 14 days, one country or one contiguous region such as the American Southwest.
Multi-country
$249 one time. Up to 21 days across two or more countries, including internal flights and rail routing.
Turnaround
5 business days for the first draft, 2 more for one round of revisions.
What you send us
Dates, departure airport, traveler count and ages, budget range, how much driving you will tolerate, and anything already booked.
What you get back
A day-by-day plan with time-stamped stops, travel times, daily costs in USD, lodging options at three price points, a packing list, and a printable PDF.
What it is not
We do not book anything on your behalf, hold your money, or act as a travel agency or seller of travel. You book everything yourself.

N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 05 · DISCLOSURE

How we make money

Five lines, in order of size. We would rather say this plainly than have you work it out from the ads.

  1. Pro subscriptions at $9 a month or $79 a year. This is the majority of our revenue and the reason the free tier can exist.
  2. Affiliate commissions from hotel, flight, rental car and tour partners. If you book through a link on this site, the partner may pay us. You pay the same price as booking direct.
  3. The custom itinerary service at $149 or $249, one time.
  4. Agency and white-label plans at $89 to $249 a month for travel agents and small tour operators.
  5. Advertising. Not running today. If we ever introduce it, units will be labeled and kept off legal pages, out of the planner workspace, and away from the top of any page.

What commission does not buy

  • It does not change the order results appear in. Sort is by distance, price or rating, and the sort you chose is always visible.
  • It does not get a hotel into an itinerary. Every lodging recommendation on this site is picked by a planner first.
  • It does not affect what a custom itinerary contains. Planners are not compensated on bookings.
  • We never sell or share your personal information. See the privacy policy.
