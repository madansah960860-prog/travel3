How it
works

Five steps, about forty-five minutes for a week-long trip. Nothing here needs a credit card and nothing gets emailed to you unless you ask.

N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 01 · STEPS 1–5

The five steps

01

Set the shape of the trip

Destination, dates in MM/DD/YYYY, number of travelers, and your departure airport. That last field matters more than people expect. A trip out of Newark and the same trip out of Boise are different trips once you price the flight and the connection.

Takes about two minutes. You can change any of it later and the plan re-prices itself.

02

Drop in the anchors first

Most trips have two or three non-negotiables. The permit you already won. The dinner reservation. The one museum. Put those on the calendar before anything else, because everything else has to bend around them.

If an anchor has a timed entry window or a lottery date, add it as a note on the stop. It shows up on the printed plan.

03

Fill the gaps with real numbers

Every stop takes a travel time from the previous stop and a cost per person. This is the unglamorous part and it is the entire reason the tool exists. A day with six stops and four hours of driving is not a day, it is a commute.

The day card totals the cost in USD and shows the total time on the move. When that number crosses five hours, cut a stop.

04

Pressure test it

Check four things before you call the day done: sunset time at that latitude in that month, opening and closing hours, any timed-entry or reservation window, and whether you have left one flexible block for weather. Trips in Iceland and the Pacific Northwest need a bigger weather buffer than trips in Arizona.

05

Pack, export, go

Pick a checklist profile that matches the trip. Cold and wet, desert heat, carry-on only, international documents. Then export. Pro members get an unlimited printable PDF, one page per day, that reads fine with no signal. Free members can buy a single export for $6.

The US traveler defaults

Hardpack assumes you are planning from the United States, so every default is set that way:

  • Prices in USD, distances in miles, temperatures in °F
  • Dates in MM/DD/YYYY
  • PTO tracker counts business days, not calendar days
  • Airport picker starts from the 42 busiest US departure airports
  • Security notes cover TSA PreCheck, CLEAR and Global Entry timing

Where the free line sits

Pricing detail

The free tier is a real product, not a trial. Two active trips, up to fourteen days each, full day-plan cards with costs, and three packing templates. No expiry, no card, no drip emails.

Pro exists for people who plan more than two trips a year, or who plan for other people. Unlimited trips, unlimited days, six collaborators, price alerts, all eleven checklist templates, and unlimited offline PDF export for $9 a month or $79 a year.

If you would rather hand the whole thing to somebody else, the custom itinerary service is a flat $149. A planner on our team builds and reviews the trip, then you own the file in your account like any other.
