Iceland 7 days

Counterclockwise around Route 1 from Keflavík. Built for September, a rental 4x4, and two travelers splitting everything.

Copy this into the planner Iceland destination guide

Trip summary

Length
7 days, 6 nights on the ground
Total driving
1,126 miles, about 25 hours across seven days
Cost per person
$1,348 on the ground, plus $420 to $780 airfare

N 64.9631 / W 19.0208 · SEC 02 · DAYS 1–7

The plan, day by day

All times are local. All costs are per person in USD and include lodging on the night you arrive.

01 Keflavík → Reykjavík Ring Road · 31 mi · 50 min driving
  • 6:40 AM Land at Keflavík (KEF)

    Overnight from Boston, Newark or Seattle. Clear passport control before 7:30 and you skip the queue that forms when three widebodies land together.

    Transit Travel 0 min $0
  • 8:30 AM Collect the rental 4x4

    Take gravel protection and the sand-and-ash waiver. Photograph every panel before you leave the lot; Icelandic rental disputes are won with timestamps.

    Transit Travel 20 min shuttle $92 per day
  • 10:15 AM Sky Lagoon

    A hot soak beats a nap you will regret at 4:00 PM. Book the first slot of the day online; walk-ups are turned away all summer.

    Activity · 2h Travel 35 min · 22 mi $69 per person
  • 1:30 PM Groceries at Bónus, Reykjavík

    Load the cooler for the week. This single stop is the difference between a $620 food budget and a $310 one.

    Supply Travel 20 min · 9 mi $46 per person
  • 3:00 PM Reykjavík on foot

    Hallgrímskirkja tower for the coloured roofs, then downhill to the old harbour. Two hours covers the whole downtown at a walk.

    Sightseeing · 2h 30m Travel 10 min $9 tower
  • 7:00 PM Dinner in Grandi and check in

    Fish stew, lamb soup, rye. Reserve two days out from June through August or you eat at 9:30 PM.

    Food and lodging Travel 10 min $166 per person
Estimated daily cost $382 per person

Highest cost day of the trip because it front-loads the lagoon, the groceries and the first rental night. It drops fast from here.

02 Reykjavík → Vík Ring Road · 183 mi · 4h 17m driving
  • 8:00 AM Thingvellir National Park

    Walk the rift between the North American and Eurasian plates. Parking is paid at a machine or online and rangers check plates.

    Sightseeing · 1h 30m Travel 55 min · 31 mi $9 parking
  • 11:00 AM Geysir geothermal field

    Strokkur erupts every six to ten minutes. Forty-five minutes covers it unless the light is worth waiting for.

    Sightseeing · 45m Travel 50 min · 37 mi $0
  • 1:30 PM Gullfoss

    Upper viewpoint when it is windy. The lower path ices from October and the railing does not inspire confidence.

    Sightseeing · 1h Travel 12 min · 6 mi $0
  • 4:30 PM Seljalandsfoss and Gljúfrabúi

    You can walk behind Seljalandsfoss and you will get soaked. Gljúfrabúi is five minutes north through a slot in the rock, and half the crowd.

    Sightseeing · 1h Travel 1h 35m · 76 mi $8 parking
  • 7:30 PM Guesthouse in Vík

    Fuel up in Hvolsvöllur, not Vík. Cheaper, and the Vík pump is often the only one open after 8:00 PM.

    Lodging Travel 45 min · 33 mi $93 per person
Estimated daily cost $164 per person

The classic Golden Circle day with a south coast tail. Four hours and change behind the wheel is the most we recommend for a sightseeing day.

03 Vík → Höfn Ring Road · 185 mi · 4h 30m driving
  • 7:45 AM Reynisfjara black sand beach

    Stay uphill of the wet sand line. Sneaker waves here arrive with no warning and have killed visitors. The signage is not decoration.

    Sightseeing · 45m Travel 12 min · 6 mi $0
  • 10:00 AM Fjaðrárgljúfur canyon

    Two miles round trip on a maintained rim path above a green slot canyon. Closed in spring while the ground thaws.

    Hike · 1h 15m Travel 1h 10m · 47 mi $0
  • 2:00 PM Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon

    Icebergs calving off Breiðamerkurjökull drift out to sea. Cross the road to Diamond Beach where they wash back up on black sand.

    Sightseeing · 1h 30m Travel 2h · 82 mi $0
  • 4:00 PM Zodiac boat among the icebergs

    May through October only, weather permitting. The last two departures sell out first because the light is best.

    Activity · 1h Travel 10 min $132 per person
  • 7:45 PM Höfn, langoustine and lodging

    The town exists for one dish and does it well. If you are turning back west here, this is the night to eat properly.

    Food and lodging Travel 1h · 50 mi $178 per person
Estimated daily cost $310 per person

The boat is the whole premium. Skip it and the day lands at $178 with no loss to the driving or the views.

04 Höfn → Egilsstaðir Ring Road · 152 mi · 3h 20m driving
  • 8:30 AM Vestrahorn at Stokksnes

    Private land with a $12 gate fee, black sand dunes and a 1,500 foot peak straight out of the sea. The best photograph in Iceland that most itineraries skip.

    Sightseeing · 1h 30m Travel 20 min · 9 mi $12 per person
  • 11:00 AM The east fjords drive

    Route 1 hugs the coast through a dozen fjords. There is no single stop here; the road is the attraction and it takes three hours with pullouts.

    Scenic drive · 3h Travel 25 min · 14 mi $0
  • 1:30 PM Lunch in Djúpivogur

    Population 450, one bakery, and a harbour where eider ducks outnumber people. Twenty minutes and you are back on the road.

    Food Travel 1h 15m · 44 mi $19 per person
  • 4:00 PM Hengifoss hike

    Two and a half miles round trip and 1,000 feet of climbing to a 420 foot waterfall banded with red clay layers. The hardest walk of the week.

    Hike · 2h 30m Travel 1h 20m · 68 mi $0
  • 7:30 PM Egilsstaðir

    The east's only real town. Supermarket, gas, laundry, and a swimming pool that costs $9 and fixes everything.

    Lodging Travel 25 min · 17 mi $88 per person
Estimated daily cost $119 per person

The quietest day of the seven and the cheapest. This is where the Ring Road stops feeling like a tour route.

05 Egilsstaðir → Mývatn Ring Road · 165 mi · 3h 05m driving
  • 8:00 AM Dettifoss, east bank

    The most powerful waterfall in Europe by volume. The east bank road, Route 864, is gravel and rough. The west bank is paved and adds 40 minutes.

    Sightseeing · 1h 15m Travel 1h 50m · 98 mi $0
  • 11:00 AM Ásbyrgi canyon

    A horseshoe canyon 300 feet deep with a birch forest inside it, which is rare enough in Iceland to be strange.

    Walk · 1h Travel 45 min · 30 mi $0
  • 1:30 PM Hverir geothermal field

    Boiling mud, sulphur vents and ground the colour of rust. It smells exactly as bad as it sounds and it is worth thirty minutes.

    Sightseeing · 45m Travel 50 min · 34 mi $0
  • 3:30 PM Mývatn Nature Baths

    The northern alternative to the Blue Lagoon at half the price and a fifth of the people. Silica-rich water at 100 to 104 °F.

    Activity · 2h Travel 15 min · 6 mi $42 per person
  • 7:00 PM Reykjahlíð for the night

    One small town on the lake shore. In summer the midges are relentless and a head net costs $6 and saves the evening.

    Lodging Travel 10 min $96 per person
Estimated daily cost $138 per person

Aurora odds are best on this night from late August onward. Mývatn is far from any city glow.

06 Mývatn → Snæfellsnes Ring Road · 292 mi · 5h 40m driving
  • 7:30 AM Goðafoss

    Twenty minutes off the road and directly beside it. The widest of the classic falls and the easiest to reach in bad weather.

    Sightseeing · 45m Travel 40 min · 32 mi $0
  • 9:30 AM Akureyri

    Iceland's second city, population 19,000. Coffee, a botanical garden that should not exist at this latitude, and the last good supermarket before the west.

    Break · 1h 30m Travel 40 min · 30 mi $22 per person
  • 12:30 PM The long west drive

    Four hours through the north-west interior with almost nothing on the road. Fuel in Blönduós and again in Borgarnes.

    Transit Travel 3h 45m · 186 mi $34 fuel per person
  • 5:00 PM Kirkjufell and Kirkjufellsfoss

    The most photographed mountain in the country, and genuinely good in evening light. Twenty minutes is enough.

    Sightseeing · 45m Travel 1h 20m · 62 mi $5 parking
  • 7:00 PM Grundarfjörður or Stykkishólmur

    Two small harbour towns fifteen minutes apart. Stykkishólmur has more rooms and better food.

    Lodging Travel 30 min · 22 mi $101 per person
Estimated daily cost $162 per person

The hard driving day. If the group is tired, add a night in Akureyri and split this across two days.

07 Snæfellsnes → Keflavík Ring Road · 148 mi · 3h 10m driving
  • 8:00 AM Snæfellsjökull peninsula loop

    Black church at Búðir, the lava fields, Arnarstapi cliffs and Djúpalónssandur beach. Three hours with stops.

    Scenic drive · 3h Travel 20 min · 12 mi $0
  • 12:00 PM Lunch in Arnarstapi

    One restaurant and a food truck in summer. Not the meal of the trip, but the cliffs behind it are.

    Food Travel 25 min · 15 mi $24 per person
  • 2:00 PM Drive south to Borgarnes

    Under the Hvalfjörður tunnel, which is tolled electronically and billed to the rental if you do not pay online within three hours.

    Transit Travel 1h 30m · 74 mi $8 toll
  • 4:00 PM Return the car at KEF

    Refuel within ten miles of the airport. Rental companies charge roughly double the pump rate to fill it for you.

    Transit Travel 1h 20m · 71 mi $41 fuel per person
  • 6:30 PM Evening flight home

    Most US-bound departures leave between 4:00 and 6:00 PM. Keflavík security is fast; two hours ahead is genuinely enough.

    Transit Travel 0 min $0
Estimated daily cost $73 per person

Budget an extra hour if you are returning the car with any damage claim to discuss. It happens more often than people expect.

N 64.9631 / W 19.0208 · SEC 04 · VARIANTS

Three ways to change it

Cut to 5 days

Run days 1 through 3, then turn around at Jökulsárlón and drive back to Reykjavík over two days with a night in Vík. Costs about $890 per person and loses almost nothing.

5 days$890 pp

Stretch to 10 days

Add a second night at Mývatn, a night in Akureyri to break day 6, and two nights on Snæfellsnes. This is the version we recommend if you can get the PTO.

10 days$1,940 pp

Winter version

Do not attempt the full loop between November and March. Run the south coast out and back over five days, add an ice cave tour, and plan around four to six hours of usable daylight.

5 days$1,120 pp
N 64.9631 / W 19.0208 · SEC 05 · BOOKING

Booking order

The sequence matters more than the timing. Book in this order and you will not paint yourself into a corner.

  1. Flights first, four to six months out. Iceland fares move less than most transatlantic routes, so there is no advantage to waiting.
  2. Lodging second, immediately after. Vík, Höfn and Mývatn have very few rooms and sell out by March for a July trip.
  3. Rental car third. Book refundable, then re-check the price monthly. Iceland rental pricing swings hard and rebooking is free.
  4. Lagoon and glacier boat last, two to four weeks out, once you know the weather pattern and can pick a slot you will actually keep.
