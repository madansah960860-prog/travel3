Cookie policy

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. It lets the site remember something between page loads, such as the fact that you are signed in or that you already answered the consent banner. This policy also covers local storage, which works similarly but stores more data and is not sent to a server automatically.

2. Consent, and what happens before you give it

On your first visit you will see a banner with three choices: Accept all, Reject non-essential, and Manage preferences. Until you choose, no analytics or advertising cookies are set. Only strictly necessary storage is active, and that is limited to remembering your consent answer and anything you have typed into the free planner or a packing checklist.

Your choice is stored for 12 months. You can change it at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page. Reopening the banner resets your consent to the strictest setting until you save a new choice.

4. Affiliate tracking

When you click an affiliate link on this site, the partner network may set a cookie in your browser so that a resulting booking is attributed to us. That cookie is set by the partner, not by us, on their domain, and typically lasts between 24 hours and 30 days depending on the network. It does not contain your name or email address. See the Affiliate Disclosure for the full explanation.

5. How to control cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies will break the sign-in on this site and will stop the free planner from remembering your trip, because both rely on strictly necessary storage.

  • Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, Third-party cookies.
  • Safari: Settings, Privacy, Manage Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data.
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions.

We honor Global Privacy Control signals sent by your browser as a valid opt-out of sale and sharing where the law requires it.

6. Changes and contact

If we add or remove a cookie category we will update this page and the effective date at the top, and the consent banner will reappear so you can make a fresh choice.
