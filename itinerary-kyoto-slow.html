Kyoto 6 days

One hotel for six nights, no repacking, and every famous stop scheduled before 8:00 AM. Built for November colour or mid-May green.

N 35.0116 / E 135.7681 · SEC 02 · DAYS 1–6

The plan, day by day

Daily costs exclude lodging after day one, which is billed as a single six-night stay.

01 Kansai Airport → downtown Kyoto Slow Kyoto · Arrival day
  • 3:40 PM Land at Kansai (KIX)

    Immigration and customs take 45 to 70 minutes on a full afternoon bank. Complete Visit Japan Web before you fly and you use the fast lane.

    Transit Travel 0 min $0
  • 5:00 PM Buy an ICOCA card and ride the Haruka

    The airport express runs to Kyoto Station in 80 minutes. Load about $60 onto the IC card; it works on buses, subways and convenience stores nationwide.

    Transit Travel 1h 20m $29 per person
  • 6:45 PM Check in downtown, Karasuma or Kawaramachi

    Central enough to walk to dinner, close enough to the subway for everything else. This is your base for all six nights.

    Lodging Travel 15 min by subway $95 per person
  • 7:45 PM Walk to Pontocho and eat

    One lane wide along the Kamo River. Jet lag is worst on night one, so eat early and do not try to stay up.

    Food Travel 12 min on foot $34 per person
  • 9:30 PM Convenience store run

    Water, breakfast, coffee. Japanese convenience store food is genuinely good and it saves an hour every morning of the trip.

    Supply Travel 5 min on foot $9 per person
Estimated daily cost $167 per person

Do not schedule anything else on arrival day. Flying west to east costs you a real day of function.

02 Higashiyama, south to north Slow Kyoto · Day 2
  • 6:30 AM Kiyomizu-dera at opening

    Opens at 6:00 AM, which is the best scheduling fact in Kyoto. By 9:30 the approach is shoulder to shoulder. At 6:30 it is you and a groundskeeper.

    Temple · 1h Travel 18 min by bus $3 entry
  • 8:00 AM Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka

    Stone stepped lanes below the temple. Shops open at 10:00, so this hour is for walking, not buying.

    Walk · 45m Travel 5 min on foot $0
  • 9:30 AM Kodai-ji

    A quieter temple with a bamboo grove nobody queues for. Ten minutes from the crowds and functionally empty.

    Temple · 1h Travel 12 min on foot $4 entry
  • 12:00 PM Nishiki Market

    Four hundred meters of covered arcade, one skewer at a time. Do not walk and eat; stand at the stall and finish there.

    Food Travel 22 min by bus $14 per person
  • 3:00 PM Ginkaku-ji and the Philosopher's Path

    Walk the canal path north to south, ending downtown. Two miles, flat, shaded, and lined with cherry trees.

    Walk · 2h Travel 25 min by bus $4 entry
  • 6:30 PM Dinner near Gion, then home

    Gion is residential. Keep your voice down, do not photograph in the marked private lanes, and be out by 9:00.

    Food Travel 20 min on foot $38 per person
Estimated daily cost $68 per person

A $5 one-day bus pass covers every transit leg on this day and pays for itself after four rides.

03 Arashiyama and the west Slow Kyoto · Day 3
  • 7:15 AM Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

    Thirty minutes of walking through the most photographed 500 meters in Japan. Before 8:00 AM it is genuinely quiet.

    Sightseeing · 45m Travel 35 min by JR $3 train
  • 8:30 AM Tenryu-ji garden

    Seven hundred years old and designed to borrow the mountain behind it. A better use of $6 than anything else in the district.

    Temple · 1h Travel 4 min on foot $6 entry
  • 10:30 AM Riverside walk to the monkey park bridge

    Free, twenty minutes each way, and the view back down the Hozu river is the one people miss because they stop at the bamboo.

    Walk · 1h 15m Travel 10 min on foot $0
  • 1:30 PM Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion

    Twenty-five minutes end to end. One viewpoint, everybody uses it, and it is still worth going.

    Temple · 45m Travel 30 min by bus $4 entry
  • 4:00 PM Nijo Castle

    Nightingale floors that squeak by design as an intruder alarm. Last entry is 4:00 PM and they enforce it.

    Castle · 1h 15m Travel 25 min by bus $9 entry
  • 7:00 PM Izakaya near Karasuma

    Small plates, cheaper than the tourist districts, and where people who work in Kyoto actually eat.

    Food Travel 15 min by subway $31 per person
Estimated daily cost $68 per person

The longest bus day of the trip. If energy is low by 3:00 PM, cut Nijo Castle rather than rushing the garden.

04 Fushimi Inari and Nara Slow Kyoto · Day 4
  • 6:00 AM Fushimi Inari Taisha

    Open 24 hours and free. Climb past the first two gate tunnels and ninety percent of visitors turn back. The full loop is 2.5 miles and 780 feet up.

    Shrine · 2h Travel 12 min by JR $3 train
  • 9:00 AM Platform breakfast at Inari

    Convenience store coffee and onigiri on the platform. This is not a compromise in Japan.

    Food Travel 8 min on foot $6 per person
  • 10:30 AM Nara, deer park and Todai-ji

    Forty-five minutes by train. Todai-ji holds the largest bronze Buddha in Japan inside one of the largest wooden buildings on earth.

    Day trip · 4h Travel 45 min by JR $18 round trip
  • 3:30 PM Nara-machi old town

    Merchant houses and small workshops, with about a third of the visitors the deer park gets.

    Walk · 1h Travel 15 min on foot $0
  • 6:45 PM Kyoto Station, ramen street

    Eleven floors of restaurants inside an architectural landmark. Ramen street is on the tenth and the queues move fast.

    Food Travel 50 min by JR $22 per person
Estimated daily cost $49 per person

The cheapest day of the six and the one most people rate highest afterwards.

05 Osaka day trip Slow Kyoto · Day 5
  • 9:00 AM Train to Osaka Umeda

    Twenty-nine minutes on the Hankyu limited express. Cheaper and more frequent than the JR route.

    Transit Travel 30 min $5 each way
  • 10:00 AM Osaka Castle and the park

    The keep is a 1931 concrete reconstruction with a museum inside. The park around it is the real reason to come.

    Sightseeing · 2h Travel 25 min by subway $5 entry
  • 1:00 PM Kuromon Ichiba market

    Osaka is a food city and this is the fastest way to understand why. Grilled scallop, takoyaki, a slice of melon.

    Food Travel 20 min by subway $26 per person
  • 3:30 PM Shinsekai and Tsutenkaku

    The scruffy south side, unchanged since the 1960s, and the best contrast to Kyoto on this whole trip.

    Walk · 1h 30m Travel 15 min by subway $8 tower
  • 6:30 PM Dotonbori at night

    Neon reflected on the canal. Loud, crowded, worth exactly one evening and no more.

    Sightseeing · 1h 30m Travel 15 min by subway $0
  • 9:00 PM Train home to Kyoto

    Last Hankyu limited express runs past 11:00 PM, so there is no rush and no risk of being stranded.

    Transit Travel 30 min $5
Estimated daily cost $54 per person

A city break inside a temple trip. If the group would rather rest, this is the day to skip entirely.

06 North Kyoto and departure Slow Kyoto · Day 6
  • 7:30 AM Send the suitcase ahead

    Takkyubin from the hotel front desk to the airport, or to your next hotel, for about $14. The single best small purchase in Japan.

    Logistics Travel 0 min $14 per person
  • 8:30 AM Kurama to Kibune hike

    An hour north by train, then a 2.5 mile forest trail over a ridge between two mountain villages. The quietest three hours of the trip.

    Hike · 3h Travel 1h by Eizan line $9 train
  • 12:30 PM Lunch in Kibune

    Riverside restaurants build platforms directly over the water in summer. Reserve, or eat at the noodle shop by the bus stop.

    Food Travel 10 min on foot $28 per person
  • 3:00 PM Last stop: Nishiki or a coffee shop

    Kyoto has a serious independent coffee scene. This is the hour for the thing you meant to do all week.

    Free time · 1h 30m Travel 50 min by train $8 per person
  • 5:30 PM Haruka express to Kansai

    Eighty minutes. Arrive at the airport three hours before an international departure; KIX check-in queues are long.

    Transit Travel 1h 20m $29 per person
Estimated daily cost $88 per person

Departure day with a real hike in it, which only works because the bag is already at the airport.

Three ways to change it

Add Tokyo, 9 days total

Fly into Haneda, four nights in Tokyo, then the Shinkansen to Kyoto for this plan minus the Osaka day. Add $190 for rail and about $520 for lodging.

9 days+$710 pp

Swap Osaka for Himeji

Himeji Castle is the finest original castle in Japan and 55 minutes each way by Shinkansen. Costs about $60 more than the Osaka day and is quieter.

Same length+$60 pp

Add a ryokan night

One night in a traditional inn with a kaiseki dinner and communal bath, ideally in Kibune or Arashiyama. Worth doing once, not for six nights.

1 night+$145 pp
N 35.0116 / E 135.7681 · SEC 05 · BOOKING

Booking order

  1. Hotel first for November. Autumn colour weekends in Kyoto book out six months ahead and rates double. For May, four months is enough.
  2. Flights second. Trans-Pacific fares are lowest 3 to 5 months out and rise sharply inside 8 weeks.
  3. Visit Japan Web third, any time before departure. It is free, it takes ten minutes, and it turns a 70 minute immigration queue into a 15 minute one.
  4. Nothing else needs booking. Temples, shrines and trains are walk-up. Only a ryokan, a tea ceremony or a kaiseki dinner need reservations.
