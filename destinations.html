N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 03 · PICKING

How to pick, if you cannot

Start from the constraint that will not move. For most US travelers that is PTO, not money.

Five days or fewer. Stay domestic or go to Mexico. A transatlantic flight burns two of five days and you will spend the trip tired.

Six to nine days. Iceland, Portugal, Kyoto, Banff, Cappadocia. One country, two bases, no internal flights.

Ten days or more. Peru, Patagonia, New Zealand, a multi-park western road trip. These need the runway and punish a compressed schedule.

Shoulder season wins almost every time. The same destination in May instead of July is typically 20 to 35 percent cheaper on lodging and considerably less crowded at the one thing everybody came to see.
