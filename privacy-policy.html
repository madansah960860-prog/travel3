2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details. Email address and a password hash. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Trip content. The destinations, dates, stops, notes and costs you enter into a trip on a Hardpack account.
  • Contact form submissions. Name, email address, optional phone number, the topic you select and the message you write.
  • Custom itinerary requests. Travel dates, departure airport, traveler count and ages, budget range, and anything else you choose to tell us.
  • Newsletter subscriptions. Email address and the date you consented.
  • Billing information. Handled by our payment processor. We receive a confirmation, the last four digits of the card and the billing country and state. We never see or store the full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. IP address, browser type and version, operating system, the page you requested, the referring page, and a timestamp.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Described in section 4 and in the Cookie Policy.
  • Local storage. If you use the free browser planner or a packing checklist without an account, your trip and your checklist progress are stored in your own browser using local storage. That data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

What we never collect

We do not ask for and do not want your passport number, government identification number, full payment card details, or precise geolocation. We do not buy personal information from data brokers.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service: saving trips, exporting PDFs, sending price alerts you asked for.
  • To answer your messages and support requests.
  • To process payments and issue refunds.
  • To send the guides email, only if you subscribed, and only until you unsubscribe.
  • To understand which pages are used, in aggregate, if you accepted analytics cookies.
  • To show advertising, if you accepted advertising cookies.
  • To detect abuse, fraud and security problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax record keeping.

We do not use your trip content to train machine learning models, and we do not read your trips except when you ask us to for a support request or a custom itinerary.

4. Cookies and tracking

We use three categories of cookie and similar storage. On your first visit a banner asks you to choose. No analytics or advertising cookies are set before you make that choice.

  • Strictly necessary. Always on. Remembers your consent choice, keeps you signed in, and stores your saved trip and checklist progress in local storage. The service cannot function without these and they do not require consent under applicable law.
  • Analytics. Optional. Counts visits and page views in aggregate so we know which guides are read to the end.
  • Advertising. Optional. Allows Google and its partners to set cookies to serve and measure advertising, including personalized advertising where permitted.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link, which appears in the footer of every page on this site. Full detail is in the Cookie Policy.

6. Advertising and how to opt out

This site does not currently display advertising. If we introduce it, ad units will be clearly labeled, and they will never appear on legal pages or the 404 page, never sit above the main content of a page, and never appear inside the planner workspace. The advertising cookie category stays switched off until you opt in either way.

To turn advertising cookies off:

  1. Open Cookie settings from the footer of any page.
  2. Untick Advertising, and Analytics if you want that off too.
  3. Save your preferences. Non-essential cookies stop immediately.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and most browsers offer a Do Not Track signal. Where required by law, including under the CPRA, we honor Global Privacy Control signals as a valid opt-out of sale and sharing.

8. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Access to production systems is limited to the two people who need it and requires multi-factor authentication. We review access quarterly.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities within the timeframes required by applicable law, and we will tell you what happened and what to do about it.

9. California privacy rights, CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights.

  • Right to know. What categories of personal information we collected, where it came from, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to.
  • Right to access. A copy of the specific pieces of personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to delete. Deletion of personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions such as tax records.
  • Right to correct. Correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. See below.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will never charge you a different price or give you a worse service for exercising any of these rights.

Do we sell or share personal information?

We do not sell personal information for money. However, the CPRA defines "sharing" broadly to include disclosing personal information to third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you consent to advertising cookies, that use may fall within the CPRA definition of sharing.

You can opt out at any time using the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link, which appears in the footer of every page of this site. Turning advertising cookies off stops any activity that could be considered sharing. We also honor Global Privacy Control browser signals as an opt-out request.

How to make a request

Email privacy@hardpack.travel with the subject line "California privacy request", or call +1 (503) 555-0182. We will acknowledge within 10 business days and respond within 45 days, extendable once by a further 45 days where permitted. We verify identity by asking you to reply from the email address on the account. An authorized agent may make a request on your behalf with written permission.

10. Other US state privacy rights

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have broadly similar rights to access, correct, delete and opt out of targeted advertising, and to appeal a refusal. Use the same contact route above and tell us your state. If we decline a request you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to the appeal within 45 days. You may also contact your state attorney general.

11. Visitors in the EU and UK

Hardpack is a US business serving US travelers, but the site is reachable worldwide. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process your personal information on these legal bases: performance of a contract for account and billing data, consent for analytics, advertising and marketing email, and legitimate interests for security and fraud prevention.

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing, and to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. Data is stored in the United States, and transfers are made under the appropriate safeguards offered by our providers, including standard contractual clauses.

12. Children's privacy, COPPA

This website and service are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. Accounts require the user to be at least 13 years old, and at least 18 to enter into a paid subscription.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email privacy@hardpack.travel and we will delete it and the associated account promptly. Parents and guardians who want to review or delete information relating to a child can use the same address.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will update the effective date at the top of this page, and we will show a notice on the site for at least 30 days. If the change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will ask for consent again where the law requires it.
