What the information on this site is, what it is not, and what you should verify yourself before you travel.

1. General information only

Everything published on hardpack.travel, including destination guides, itineraries, budget tables, packing checklists and blog articles, is provided for general informational purposes. It is not professional advice of any kind. It is not travel agency advice, legal advice, immigration advice, medical advice, financial advice or insurance advice.

Hardpack Trip Co. is a software company that publishes editorial content. We are not a travel agency, a tour operator, a seller of travel, an air carrier or a licensed insurance producer.

2. Prices, hours and rules change

Every price, opening hour, entrance fee, permit rule, drive time and travel requirement on this site was accurate to the best of our knowledge on the review date printed at the top of that page. Travel information changes constantly and often without notice.

Before you rely on anything time-critical, verify it with the official source. That means the operator's own website, the National Park Service at nps.gov, Recreation.gov, the airline, the embassy or consulate, and travel.state.gov for entry requirements.

3. Cost estimates are estimates

Budget figures on this site are our own planning estimates, built from trips we have planned and taken. They assume specific conditions, usually two travelers sharing a room and a vehicle, mid-range lodging, and a stated season. Your costs will differ. Exchange rates, fuel prices, seasonal demand, group size and your own choices all move the number.

Nothing on this site is a quote, an offer, or a guarantee of price or availability.

4. Safety information

Safety notes on our destination pages describe hazards we consider significant. They are not a complete list of every risk, and they are not a substitute for current local advice, ranger guidance, posted signage, or your own judgment on the day.

Outdoor activity carries inherent risk. Hiking, driving in unfamiliar conditions, cold water, altitude, heat, wildlife and weather can injure or kill people who are prepared, let alone people who are not. You are responsible for assessing your own fitness and experience against any activity we describe, and for turning back when conditions say so.

In an emergency, call the local emergency number. In the United States that is 911. Numbers for other countries are printed on the relevant destination page.

5. Health, vaccination and insurance

We are not medical professionals. Consult a doctor or a travel health clinic about vaccinations, medication, altitude, and any condition that could be affected by travel. Check the CDC travel health pages for current guidance.

Most US health plans provide limited or no coverage outside the United States, and Medicare generally provides none. We describe travel insurance in general terms only. We do not assess your needs, recommend a policy, or act as your agent. Read the policy documents and, if you need advice, talk to a licensed producer.

6. Third-party websites and suppliers

This site links to third-party websites. We do not control them and we are not responsible for their content, accuracy, prices, availability, security or practices. Any booking you make with a supplier is a contract between you and that supplier, on their terms. Disputes about a booking are between you and them.

Some outbound links are affiliate links. See the Affiliate Disclosure.

7. No guarantee of outcome

Using Hardpack does not guarantee that a trip will go to plan, that a permit lottery will be won, that a road will be open, that a flight will operate, or that you will be admitted to any country. Weather, government decisions, supplier failures and simple bad luck are outside anyone's control, including ours.

8. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Hardpack Trip Co. accepts no liability for loss, injury, cost or inconvenience arising from your use of information published on this site. The full limitation is in section 11 of the Terms of Service.

9. Corrections

If something on this site is wrong, tell us. Email hello@hardpack.travel with the page and the correction. We fix errors quickly and we note significant corrections on the page itself.
