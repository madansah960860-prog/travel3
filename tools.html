N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 03 · METHOD

How to actually use a packing list

Pack from the list, not from memory

Lay everything out first, then tick. The point of the list is not to remind you of the obvious things, it is to catch the four items you forget every single trip. For most people that is the adapter, the medication, the second card and the headlamp.

Cut, then cut again

These lists are the maximum, not the target. Anything you did not use last trip comes off this trip. The single heaviest thing most travelers carry is a second pair of shoes they never wear.

Print the day before

Use the print button on any list. It prints with the grid, the ads and the navigation stripped out, so you get a clean page you can mark up with a pen and leave on the bed while you pack.
