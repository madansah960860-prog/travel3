Utah 5 days

Las Vegas in and out. Five national parks, 912 miles, and the Arches timed entry handled by leaving before 7:00 AM.

Copy this into the planner Permits and timed entry guide

N 38.5733 / W 109.5498 · SEC 02 · DAYS 1–5

The plan, day by day

Costs are per person in USD with two travelers sharing one vehicle and one room.

01 Las Vegas → Springdale Mighty 5 · 174 mi · 3h 25m driving
  • 7:30 AM Collect the rental at LAS

    Mid-size SUV, not a compact. You are carrying water, a cooler and gear, and clearance helps on unpaved pullouts.

    Transit Travel 25 min shuttle $62 per day
  • 9:00 AM Groceries and water, North Las Vegas

    Last full supermarket for 160 miles. One gallon of water per person per day, a bag of ice, a foam cooler.

    Supply Travel 20 min · 12 mi $34 per person
  • 12:45 PM Zion visitor center

    Buy the $80 America the Beautiful annual pass here. It covers all five parks and pays for itself at park three.

    Park entry Travel 2h 40m · 162 mi $40 per person
  • 2:00 PM Riverside Walk by shuttle

    The canyon road is shuttle only March through November. Riverside Walk is 2.2 flat paved miles to the mouth of the Narrows.

    Hike · 2h Travel 40 min shuttle $0
  • 6:30 PM Springdale for the night

    At the park gate and walkable to the shuttle. Rooms run $190 to $310 in season and sell out by April.

    Lodging Travel 15 min $112 per person
Estimated daily cost $186 per person

The annual pass is a one-time cost booked to day one and used for the whole trip. Split two ways it is $40 each.

02 Zion → Bryce Canyon Mighty 5 · 86 mi · 2h 10m driving
  • 5:45 AM Angels Landing, permit required

    The chain section needs a seasonal or day-before lottery permit. Without one, hike to Scout Lookout: same view, minus the last half mile.

    Hike · 4h Travel 30 min shuttle $6 lottery fee
  • 11:00 AM Back to Springdale, repack

    Get out of the canyon before midday. In July the shuttle stops read 100 °F by noon.

    Break Travel 40 min $18 per person
  • 1:00 PM Mount Carmel Highway

    Route 9 climbs the switchbacks and through the 1930 tunnel. Vehicles over 7 feet 10 inches wide need a paid escort.

    Scenic drive · 1h 30m Travel 1h 45m · 72 mi $0
  • 4:00 PM Bryce rim, Sunset to Sunrise Point

    One flat mile with the best hoodoo views in the park. Bryce sits at 8,000 feet and runs 20 °F cooler than Zion.

    Walk · 1h 30m Travel 25 min · 14 mi $0 with pass
  • 8:30 PM Ranger astronomy program

    One of the darkest certified skies in the lower 48. Free, most summer evenings, first come.

    Activity · 1h Travel 10 min $0
Estimated daily cost $119 per person

Lodging in Tropic, fifteen minutes east, runs about $95 per person when the in-park lodge is gone.

03 Bryce → Capitol Reef → Moab Mighty 5 · 272 mi · 5h 30m driving
  • 7:00 AM Sunrise at Bryce Point

    Twelve minutes from the lodge. Bring a jacket; it can be 38 °F at sunrise in June at 8,300 feet.

    Sightseeing · 1h Travel 12 min · 5 mi $0 with pass
  • 9:30 AM Highway 12 Scenic Byway

    One of the best drives in the country. The Hogback has drop-offs on both sides and no shoulder. Take it slow, use the pullouts.

    Scenic drive · 2h 30m Travel 2h 30m · 112 mi $0
  • 12:30 PM Capitol Reef, Fruita

    The least visited of the five and the only one with a pie shop. The eight mile Scenic Drive adds an hour.

    Park · 2h Travel 20 min · 9 mi $0 with pass
  • 4:00 PM Drive to Moab

    Fill the tank in Hanksville. The next reliable station is 68 miles on and closes early.

    Transit Travel 2h 30m · 146 mi $29 fuel per person
  • 7:30 PM Moab for two nights

    Base for Arches and Canyonlands. $170 to $280 in spring and fall, and it drops hard in July when it hits 104 °F.

    Lodging Travel 10 min $108 per person
Estimated daily cost $161 per person

The driving day. 272 miles and five and a half hours is our ceiling for a single day and this hits it.

04 Arches National Park Mighty 5 · 62 mi · 1h 40m driving
  • 6:15 AM Enter before the timed entry window

    Reservations are required 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM April through October. Arrive before 7:00 and the requirement does not apply.

    Park entry Travel 12 min · 6 mi $0 with pass
  • 6:40 AM Delicate Arch at sunrise

    Three miles round trip, 480 feet of climbing, no shade on the slickrock. Headlamp for the first twenty minutes.

    Hike · 2h 15m Travel 22 min · 12 mi $0
  • 9:30 AM Windows Section loop

    Three arches in a flat mile. The best value per step in the park and done before the heat arrives.

    Walk · 1h Travel 14 min · 7 mi $0
  • 12:15 PM Lunch and shade in Moab

    Midday in the desert is for shade, not sightseeing. Refill four liters per person before you head back out.

    Food Travel 18 min · 10 mi $21 per person
  • 5:45 PM Dead Horse Point sunset

    A state park with a separate fee and a better sunset than the crowded overlooks inside Arches. Wind picks up hard on the rim.

    Sightseeing · 1h 30m Travel 45 min · 27 mi $10 per person
Estimated daily cost $96 per person

Lodging counted on the night you arrive, so day four carries no room charge. Two travelers splitting one vehicle.

05 Canyonlands → Las Vegas Mighty 5 · 318 mi · 5h 45m driving
  • 6:30 AM Mesa Arch at sunrise

    Half a mile round trip. Every photographer in Moab is there, and the ten minutes when the underside of the arch glows are still worth it.

    Sightseeing · 1h Travel 40 min · 32 mi $0 with pass
  • 8:00 AM Grand View Point

    The overlook that explains the whole park. Two miles round trip on the rim trail if you have the legs.

    Walk · 1h 30m Travel 25 min · 12 mi $0 with pass
  • 10:30 AM Back to Moab, pack and fuel

    Top up here. I-70 west has long gaps between stations and the prices are worse.

    Break Travel 45 min · 34 mi $26 fuel per person
  • 12:00 PM I-70 west and I-15 south

    Five hours of interstate. Salina and St George are the two sensible stops.

    Transit Travel 5h · 240 mi $16 per person
  • 5:30 PM Return the car at LAS

    Refuel within five miles of the airport. Rental companies charge roughly double to fill it for you.

    Transit Travel 20 min $0
Estimated daily cost $42 per person

Fly out after 8:00 PM to be safe. If your flight is earlier, drive to St George on day four evening and cut the morning short.

N 38.5733 / W 109.5498 · SEC 04 · VARIANTS

Three ways to change it

Add the Grand Canyon, 7 days

Drop south from Bryce to the North Rim, which is open mid-May through mid-October and gets a fraction of the South Rim traffic. Adds two days and about $260 per person.

7 days+$260 pp

Camp it, 5 days

Watchman in Zion, North Campground at Bryce, Devils Garden at Arches. Book six months out at 10:00 AM Eastern on Recreation.gov. Saves $430 per person.

5 days$1,110 pp

Winter version, 4 days

Zion and Bryce only, December through February. Snow on the hoodoos, no shuttle, no timed entry, and lodging at half price. Roads to Capitol Reef and Highway 12 are unreliable.

4 days$920 pp
N 38.5733 / W 109.5498 · SEC 05 · BOOKING

Booking order

  1. Lodging first. Springdale and Moab in April, May, September and October sell out four to six months ahead and prices climb the whole way.
  2. Angels Landing lottery second. There is a seasonal lottery months ahead and a day-before lottery that closes at 3:00 PM Mountain. Enter both. $6 to apply, $3 more if you win.
  3. Arches timed entry third, released on Recreation.gov three months ahead plus a next-day block at 7:00 PM Mountain. Or skip it entirely by entering before 7:00 AM.
  4. Rental car and flights last. Las Vegas has enough supply of both that waiting costs you very little.
