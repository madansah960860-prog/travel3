Refund and Cancellation Policy

When you get money back, how much, how long it takes, and the two situations where we cannot refund.

Effective date: 06/01/2026 Last updated: 06/01/2026 Hardpack Trip Co. Portland, Oregon, USA

Please note: this is a starting template written for our business. Have a licensed attorney review and adapt it before relying on it for yours. Questions about this page go to hello@hardpack.travel.

N 45.5152 / W 122.6784 · SEC 01 · REFUNDS

Full text

The short version

  • New Pro subscription: full refund within 14 days of the first charge.
  • Annual plan after 14 days: prorated refund for complete unused months.
  • Custom itinerary: full refund any time before we start work, and a full refund within 7 days of delivery if the revised plan still does not work for you.
  • Agency plans: cancel any time, access runs to the end of the paid month.

1. Pro subscriptions

The 14 day window

If you are charged for a Pro subscription for the first time and change your mind, email billing@hardpack.travel within 14 days of that charge and we will refund it in full. We do not ask why, and we do not run a retention script at you.

Monthly plans after 14 days

Monthly subscriptions are not refunded after the 14 day window, because the month has largely been used. Cancel at any time and you keep full access until the end of the period you paid for. No further charge is taken.

Annual plans after 14 days

Annual subscriptions are refunded on a prorated basis for complete unused months, calculated from the date we receive your cancellation. For example, cancelling a $79 annual plan after 4 months and 12 days refunds 7 complete unused months, which is $46.

Renewals

We email a reminder before every annual renewal. If a renewal charge takes you by surprise, contact us within 14 days and we will refund it in full and cancel the plan. This applies once per account.

2. Custom itinerary service

The custom itinerary service is $149 for a single country or region and $249 for a multi-country plan, charged once at the time of order.

  • Before we start work: full refund, no conditions. We tell you by email when a planner has been assigned, which is the point work starts.
  • After delivery: one round of revisions is included. If the revised plan still does not meet the brief you gave us, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we refund the fee in full. You keep the file.
  • After 7 days: the service has been delivered and consumed, so it is not refundable. If something is factually wrong in the plan we will correct it at no charge at any time.

3. Agency plans

Studio and Outfitter plans are billed monthly with no minimum term. Cancel at any time from your account or by emailing agents@hardpack.travel. Access and client-facing links continue to the end of the month you have paid for.

Partial months are not refunded, because seats and white-label hosting are provisioned for the full period. Enterprise agreements follow the cancellation terms in the signed contract.

4. Add-on purchases

The $6 single PDF export is a one-time digital delivery. It is refundable only if the export fails to generate or is corrupted, in which case we refund it or fix it, whichever you prefer.

5. Where we cannot refund

  • Travel costs. We do not sell travel and we never hold money for a supplier. A refund for a flight, hotel, tour or rental car is between you and that supplier, under their terms. We can help you find the right contact, but we cannot refund what we never took.
  • Accounts terminated for a material breach of the Terms of Service, such as scraping the site at scale or reselling access. Fees already paid are not refunded in that case.

6. How to request a refund

  1. Email billing@hardpack.travel from the address on the account.
  2. Tell us which charge, and the date. You do not need to give a reason.
  3. We acknowledge within 1 business day.
  4. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Your bank typically posts it within 5 to 10 business days after that.

You can also call +1 (503) 555-0182, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific.

7. What happens to your data after cancellation

Nothing is deleted. Your trips become read-only and stay printable and exportable for as long as your account exists. If you want the account and its data deleted entirely, ask, and we will do it within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.

8. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, reply to our email and ask for it to be reviewed by a founder. We respond within 5 business days. Please talk to us before filing a chargeback; a chargeback costs us a fee and takes far longer to resolve than an email does.
