How to use somebody else's itinerary

Copy it, then cut a third

Every published plan is written at the pace of somebody who has been there before. On your first visit, remove one stop a day. You will still see more than you planned to.

Check the month

These plans are built for a specific season. The Iceland plan assumes fourteen hours of daylight. Run it in November and half the stops happen in the dark.

Reprice it for your group

All costs assume two people sharing. Solo travelers should add roughly 40 percent to lodging. Groups of four usually save 15 to 20 percent per person on vehicles and rentals.
